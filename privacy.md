# Privacy Policy for DynaWeather

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

**Szaniszló Miklós E.V.** ("we", "our", or "us"), operating under the developer brand **CraftedBytes**, built the **DynaWeather** mobile application (the "Service") as a freemium and ad-supported application. This page is used to inform visitors and users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decides to use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while using our Service, we may require you to provide or allow access to certain limited information. We do not collect or store personally identifiable information (such as your name, address, or phone number) on our own servers.

### A. Approximate Location Data
* **Purpose:** To display real-time weather effects (such as rain or snow) that match your local environment, the app may request access to your device's approximate location (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`).
* **Processing:** Your approximate coordinates (latitude and longitude) are processed strictly on your device and are transmitted solely to our third-party weather data provider (MET Norway) to fetch local meteorological forecasts.
* **Storage:** We do not store your location history on external servers. You can decline location permissions at any time and manually select a city from our built-in offline database instead.

### B. User Photos and Media
* **Purpose:** DynaWeather allows you to select personal photos from your device's gallery to use as a dynamic wallpaper background.
* **Processing:** All image processing and graphical rendering (via fragment shaders) happen 100% locally on your device's GPU. Your personal photos are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or any third party.

## 3. Log Data and Crash Reporting

In case of an error or crash in the app, we may collect diagnostic data and crash logs (via Google Play Console / Android Vitals). This data may include information such as your device model, operating system version, app configuration, and the time and date of the error, used solely to improve app stability and performance.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
